# Secondary Persona: Sandra, the Rookie Organizer

*If there's another important user type, define them briefly.*

**Persona Name:** Sandra, the rookie organizer of a conference

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age & Demographics** | 28-35 years old, urban professional |
| **Role/Job Title** | Mid-level Data Analyst / First-time Conference Organizer |
| **Experience Level** | 3-5 years in data analysis, intermediate tech skills, new to conference logistics |
| **Work Environment** | Hybrid office, uses laptop and desktop, often in meetings |

## Goals & Motivations
*What does this persona want to achieve?*
- **Primary Goal:** Generate accurate reports quickly without manual data manipulation
- **Secondary Goal:** Gain credibility with stakeholders through reliable insights
- **What motivates them:** Recognition for delivering high-quality work efficiently

## Pain Points & Frustrations
*What problems do they currently face?*
- ❌ **Pain 1:** Spending hours manually combining data from different sources
- ❌ **Pain 2:** Data inconsistencies leading to errors in reports
- ❌ **Pain 3:** Lack of confidence in the tools they currently use

## Needs & Expectations
*What does this persona need from our product?*
- ✅ **Need 1:** A single platform that integrates all data sources
- ✅ **Need 2:** Automated data validation to catch errors early
- ✅ **Need 3:** Simple, intuitive interface that doesn't require training

## Tech Savviness
- [ ] Beginner (needs hand-holding, prefers simple interfaces)
- [X] Intermediate (comfortable with standard tools, learns quickly)
- [ ] Advanced (power user, wants customization and shortcuts)

## Quote
> *"I just need to see the data, not spend half my day fighting with it."*
