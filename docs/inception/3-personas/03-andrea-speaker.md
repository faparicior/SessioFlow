# Stakeholder Persona: Andrea, the Experienced Speaker

**Persona Name:** Andrea, the experienced speaker

| Attribute | Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Age & Demographics** | 32 years, female, urban |
| **Role/Job Title** | Developer and Agile enthusiast |
| **Experience Level** | 6 years of experience in software development |
| **Work Environment** | Full remote. With Scrum and daily meetings |

## Goals & Motivations
*What does this persona want to achieve?*
- **Primary Goal:** Have easy way to propose her own sessions.
- **Secondary Goal:** Have a clear view of the schedule and steps in the process.
- **What motivates them:** Share knowledge to the community through talks and gain relevance in the community.

## Pain Points & Frustrations
*What problems do they currently face?*
- ❌ **Pain 1:** Difficult to find a smooth an easy way to create a session proposal.
- ❌ **Pain 2:** Add a partner to the proposal is not easy.
- ❌ **Pain 3:** Have information about the conference, recommendations about travel discounts and accommodations is not easy.

## Needs & Expectations
*What does this persona need from our product?*
- ✅ **Need 1:** A single point with all information about the conference, recommendations about travel discounts and accommodations.
- ✅ **Need 2:** An easy way to add a colleague to the proposal.
- ✅ **Need 3:** Simple, intuitive interface that doesn't require training.

- [ ] Beginner (needs hand-holding, prefers simple interfaces)
- [X] Intermediate (comfortable with standard tools, learns quickly)
- [ ] Advanced (power user, wants customization and shortcuts)
